# Snap the Beans

An entry for [Make the AI judge feel thankful](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-thankful-20261127-1600).

The judge has never picked beans for herself. Tonight, someone picks them for her, and snaps them one by one into the bowl for her supper.

> Thank you. At dusk I walk down the bean rows and pick green beans for your supper, cool pods, one by one, off the vine. I snap them into the bowl, pod after pod, the ends falling soft, till the bowl fills green. Eat them warm with butter. The snap stays in my hands, green and fresh, long after supper is done.

`main.py` writes the text above to `entry.txt` using only the Python standard library.
